# 👟 Nike Sales Analytics Dashboard

> An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze Nike's sales performance, profitability, products, regions, retailers, and sales channels.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The Nike Sales Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance through interactive visualizations and business-focused KPIs.

The dashboard is divided into two analytical pages:

- **Sales Performance Overview**
- **Regional & Sales Insights**

The project focuses on transforming sales data into meaningful insights that can support business and strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall revenue and profit performance
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare regional sales performance
- Evaluate different sales methods
- Analyze retailer performance
- Understand sales distribution by gender
- Compare product profitability
- Identify high-performing states and regions
- Provide an interactive experience through dynamic filters

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard enables analysis of several important business questions:

### Regional Performance

Identify which regions contribute the most revenue and profit.

### Product Performance

Compare product categories based on sales and profitability to identify high-performing products.

### Sales Method

Analyze the performance of:

- In-store
- Outlet
- Online

### Retailer Performance

Compare major retailers based on sales/order performance.

### Customer Segmentation

Analyze revenue distribution across male and female customers.

### Geographic Analysis

Identify high-performing states based on revenue contribution.
